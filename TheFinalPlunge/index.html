Engine Highlights

Built for True Space Exploration

Engineered from the ground up for physical realism, responsive controls, and modular depth.

Fixed-Epoch Keplerian Physics

Zero energy drift propagation relative to dominant celestial bodies. Patched conics, delta-v budgeting, and realistic gravitational Sphere of Influence (SOI) transitions.

Modular Vehicle Assembly (VAB)

Snap-grid part engineering with dynamic multi-part physics. Calculates real-time Center of Mass (CoM), Center of Thrust (CoT), staging sequences, and cross-feed fuel routing.

Maneuver Nodes & Navigation

Plan trajectory transfers with prograde, retrograde, and radial burn vectors. Interactive orbital curves predict your intercept and escape velocities dynamically.

Co-op & Docking Architecture

Engineered with multiplayer peer-to-peer telemetry sync in mind. Modular docking ports, craft rendezvous procedures, and multi-vessel tracking across the solar system.
